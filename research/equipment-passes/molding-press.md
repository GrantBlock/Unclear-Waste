# Molding Press — Specialty Equipment Pass — Pass date: 2026-08-28

Tracker line items #5 "Molding Hopper" and #6 "Molding Press" — both currently unsourced ("-" on
FROM/TO). The line layout around these items (a molding hopper feeding the press, then gravity
roller conveyors for "Empty Mold Transfer" in both directions, #7/#8) points to a **flaskless
matchplate molding machine**: it makes a self-contained cope-and-drag mold that rides out on a
conveyor with no permanent flask attached, which is why there are two identical "Empty Mold
Transfer Conveyor" line items rather than a flask-return loop.

## Recommendation for next step

Get quotes from both DISA and Hunter — both are flaskless matchplate, both fit the existing
gravity-roller mold conveyor layout without redesign. DISA has the Norican/Simpson relationship
in its favor; Hunter has a longer specific track record in aluminum jobbing work and is
US-based, which likely means shorter lead time and easier field service than a Danish OEM. If
EMI/Savelli comes up in the RFQ, confirm their molding approach (tight-flask vs. flaskless)
before treating it as a drop-in option — it may require reworking the mold-handling conveyors.

Pin down mold size (flask/matchplate dimensions) and target molds/hour before requesting quotes
— those numbers weren't in the tracker and are the primary drivers of press sizing and price.
