## What mulling does that a mixer doesn't

Mulling is a specific, more intensive form of sand mixing: muller wheels press the sand against
the chamber wall while plows shear and blend it, applying compression and controlled shear
rather than just stirring components together. That shear/compression action is what fully
develops the sand-clay-additive bond — a plain paddle mixer doesn't get there. For a foundry,
that shows up as lower sand temperature, more consistent moisture and compactability run to run,
and less scrap from bad sand. Per ton mixed, mullers also use less new sand, power, and bentonite
than turbine-style mixers — relevant since sand and bentonite are recurring consumables on a
green sand line.
